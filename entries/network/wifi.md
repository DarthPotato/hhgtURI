# The WiFi

::: {custom-style="Deck"}
Three networks, one of which you want, and a login that quietly expires.
:::

Campus runs three networks, and the names tell you less than you'd hope [VERIFIED — its.uri.edu]. **URI_Secure** is the one you want: encrypted, logged in with your URI account, the network the university would like everyone on. **URI_Open** is the lobby. It exists so you can get onto URI_Secure, and so guests can get online. **eduroam** is for visitors from other schools.

To set up for the first time, or when your connection stops working for no reason you can see: delete any old URI_Secure or eduroam profile from your device, join URI_Open, go to **rhodywifi.uri.edu**, and follow the instructions for your device [VERIFIED — its.uri.edu].

That second situation will happen to you. The setup doesn't last forever. It runs out after about a year [ORAL — author, ITS desk shifts], at a moment of its own choosing, and the fix is the same as the setup. Congratulations on having done it before.

Some sites are blocked on the campus network [ORAL — author, ITS desk shifts]. This book will not be helping with that.
